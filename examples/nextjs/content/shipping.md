# Shipping and delivery

## When your order ships

Roasting happens Monday, Wednesday and Friday morning. Orders placed before 6am
on a roast day ship the same afternoon; anything later goes out on the next
roast day. We do this so beans reach you within 48 hours of roasting rather than
sitting in a warehouse.

## Delivery times

| Destination | Carrier | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | Royal Mail Tracked 24 | 1 to 2 working days |
| Ireland and EU | An Post / DPD | 3 to 5 working days |
| United States | DHL Express | 4 to 7 working days |
| Rest of world | DHL Express | 7 to 14 working days |

## Shipping cost

UK delivery is £3.95, and free on orders over £30. EU delivery is £8.50 flat.
United States delivery is £12 flat. Subscriptions always ship free, on every
destination, from the first box onward.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel is collected, not when the order is
placed. If the tracking has not moved after two working days, contact us and we
will open a case with the carrier.

## Customs and duties

Orders outside the UK may attract import duty. We declare the true value on the
customs form and cannot mark parcels as gifts. Duty is paid by the recipient.
