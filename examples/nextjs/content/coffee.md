# Coffee, grind and storage

## Choosing a grind

We grind to order. Choose whole bean if you own a grinder, because coffee stales
several times faster once ground.

- Espresso: fine, like table salt
- Moka pot and Aeropress: medium-fine
- Pour over, V60 and Chemex: medium
- Cafetiere and French press: coarse, like raw sugar
- Cold brew: extra coarse

## How long the coffee stays good

Beans are at their best between 5 and 21 days after the roast date printed on
the bag. They are perfectly drinkable for about three months. Ground coffee
should be used within two weeks.

## Storing it properly

Keep the bag sealed, at room temperature, away from light. The valve on the bag
lets carbon dioxide out without letting oxygen in, so the original bag is better
than most containers. Do not refrigerate: coffee absorbs smells and condensation
ruins it. Freezing works only for unopened bags you will not touch for months.

## Decaf

Our decaf is Swiss Water processed, which uses no chemical solvents. It is
roasted on the same days as everything else and ships with the same guarantee.

## Allergens

Coffee is roasted in a facility that handles no nuts, dairy or gluten. Flavoured
coffees are not something we sell.
