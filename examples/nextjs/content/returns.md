# Returns and refunds

## The freshness guarantee

If you do not like the coffee, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will
refund the order in full. You do not need to send the beans back, and you do not
need a reason. We would rather lose the sale than have you drink coffee you do
not enjoy.

## How to start a refund

Reply to your order confirmation email with the word "refund", or use the
contact form with your order number. Refunds are approved by a person, usually
within one working day, and land back on the original payment method in three to
five working days.

## What is not covered

Wholesale orders over 5kg are final sale, because they are roasted to order in
quantities we cannot resell. Gift cards are non-refundable but never expire.
Equipment such as grinders and brewers can be returned unused within 14 days.

## Damaged or wrong items

Send a photo of the bag and the packaging. We ship a replacement the same roast
day and do not ask for the original back.
