# Wholesale and offices

## Who wholesale is for

Cafes, restaurants, offices and anyone using more than 5kg a month. Pricing
starts at £16 per kilogram and drops at 20kg and 50kg per month.

## Getting started

Email wholesale with your location, the equipment you brew on, and roughly how
much coffee you go through in a week. We send samples of three roasts free of
charge, and a dialling-in visit is included for accounts within the M25.

## Wholesale terms

Accounts are invoiced monthly on 30-day terms after the first three orders,
which are paid up front. Wholesale orders are roasted to order and are not
refundable, though we will replace anything that arrives damaged or stale.

## Office coffee

Offices under 5kg a month are better served by a large subscription, which is
cheaper per bag and needs no account setup. We can invoice a company rather than
charging a card if that is easier for your finance team.
