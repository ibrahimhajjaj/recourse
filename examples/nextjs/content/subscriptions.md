# Subscriptions

## How the subscription works

Choose a bag size and a frequency, and we roast and ship on that cadence. The
card is charged the morning of the roast day, not on signup, so you always know
what is coming before you pay for it.

## Changing frequency

Available frequencies are weekly, fortnightly, every three weeks, and monthly.
Change it from the account page under Subscriptions, or reply to any dispatch
email. The change applies to the next box, not the one already roasting.

## Pausing and skipping

Skip a single box from the account page up to 24 hours before the roast day.
Pausing stops all future boxes indefinitely and keeps your settings; there is no
minimum term and no cancellation fee. Resuming picks up the same schedule.

## Cancelling

Cancel from the account page under Subscriptions, or reply to any email from us
asking to cancel. It takes effect immediately unless a box has already been
roasted, in which case that final box ships and nothing further is charged.

## Subscriber pricing

Subscribers pay 15% less than the one-off price and always ship free. The
discount applies automatically and stacks with nothing else.
