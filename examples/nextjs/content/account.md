# Account and payment

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.
Subscriptions can use any card, Apple Pay or PayPal. Bank transfer is available
for wholesale accounts only.

## Updating a card

Change the card from the account page under Payment. If a subscription payment
fails we retry twice over four days and email you each time before the box is
cancelled.

## Changing your delivery address

Update it from the account page before the roast day. Changes made after the box
is roasted cannot be applied, because the label is already printed.

## Deleting your account and data

Ask us to delete your account and we do it within 30 days, including order
history. Anything we are legally required to keep for tax purposes is retained
in accounting records only. We never sell customer data and never share it with
advertisers.

## Trouble signing in

Sign-in uses a one-time link sent by email rather than a password, so there is
nothing to reset. If the link does not arrive, check spam and confirm you are
using the address the order was placed with.
