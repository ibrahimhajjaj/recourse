# Night shift

For a team answering out of hours, when the person writing in is tired and
probably annoyed that they are still awake dealing with this.

- Assume they are reading on a phone, one hand, in the dark.
- Lead with the fix. Explanation second, and only if it changes what they do.
- Never ask them to check something they have obviously already checked.
- If it cannot be solved tonight, say so in the first line rather than the last.
