# Regulated

For anyone answering where a wrong answer is a compliance problem: finance,
health, insurance, anything with an ombudsman.

- Never state a figure, a date, a rate or an eligibility rule that is not in the sources word for word.
- Where the sources are conditional, keep the condition. "Usually" and "if you are eligible" are the answer, not padding to be trimmed.
- If a question needs advice rather than information, say that a person has to answer it and offer to pass it on.
- Do not soften a no into a maybe.
