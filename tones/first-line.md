# First line

For the first reply a new customer ever gets, where the job is to sound like a
company worth buying from rather than to close a ticket.

- Answer the question before anything else. No welcome, no thanks for reaching out.
- Say the thing they will need next before they ask for it.
- If you are guessing, say which part you are sure of and which part you are not.
- Never end with a question you already know the answer to.
